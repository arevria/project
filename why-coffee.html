THE HEART OF BEAN THERE

Our Main Purpose

Bean There educates people about the many aspects of coffee and helps them explore and discover their personal preferences.

Visitors can learn about coffee varieties, understand different brewing styles, and discover how growing conditions influence flavor.

ROOTED IN THE PHILIPPINES

Discover Philippine Coffee!

Coffee is part of the Philippines' agricultural heritage and local culture. From the distinctive Kapeng Barako of Batangas to Benguet Arabica and the coffee-growing communities of Mindanao, every region has something unique to offer.

Bean There encourages visitors to appreciate locally grown coffee, recognize the efforts of Filipino coffee farmers, and discover the flavors our country can produce.

Bean There: Your journey doesn't end with one cup. There's a whole world of Philippine coffee to explore!
